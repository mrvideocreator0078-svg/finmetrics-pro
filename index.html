Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the compound interest calculator?
Our calculator implements the standard financial formula A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) + PMT × [((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n)] with monthly compounding frequency. It factors in realistic continuous compounding effects and provides an optional real purchasing power discount rate based on historical inflation data.
Why make extra mortgage payments toward principal?
During the initial 10 to 15 years of a standard 30-year mortgage, the vast majority of your monthly payment goes toward bank interest rather than principal. Any extra principal payment directly reduces the underlying balance upon which future interest is calculated, generating guaranteed compounding interest savings.
Can I export reports and amortization tables to CSV or PDF?
Yes. Every flagship calculator includes one-click CSV spreadsheet export buttons and optimized print formatting for saving clean, ad-free PDF audit reports directly to your local drive.
