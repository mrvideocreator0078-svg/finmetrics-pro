Frequently Asked Questions

How does the FinMetrics compound interest engine calculate returns?
Our calculator implements the standard future value formula:

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) + PMT × [ ((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n) ]

Where A is the final portfolio value, P is the initial investment, r is the nominal annual return rate, n is the compounding frequency per year, and t is the investment duration in years. We also provide an inflation-adjusted calculation discounting future cash flows by historical purchasing power indices.
Why make extra mortgage payments directly toward principal?
During the initial 10 to 15 years of a standard 30-year mortgage, the vast majority of your monthly payment goes toward bank interest rather than principal reduction. Applying an extra principal payment immediately decreases the outstanding balance upon which subsequent interest is calculated, producing guaranteed compounded savings across every remaining month of the mortgage.
Is any of my financial data tracked or transmitted?
Zero. All calculations are executed 100% client-side inside your browser's local JavaScript runtime. No balance figures, salaries, home values, or debt numbers are ever transmitted across the network or stored in external databases.
Can I export my results to CSV or print a report?
Yes. Every flagship calculator includes one-click CSV spreadsheet export buttons and optimized print formatting for saving clean, ad-free PDF audit reports directly to your local drive.
