Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At FinMetrics Pro, your privacy is our foundational principle. Because all mathematical models and simulations operate 100% client-side inside your web browser, we do not collect, process, transmit, or monetize any financial numbers, debt balances, salaries, or property values that you enter into our tools.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal identification data (no required user registration, names, phone numbers, or physical addresses).
  • No financial account numbers, credit card numbers, or bank login credentials.
  • No calculation logs: All numbers typed into input fields or sliders remain in your device's memory.

2. Browser Local Storage

FinMetrics Pro utilizes your web browser's local storage solely to remember your preferred visual theme (Dark Mode vs. Light Mode) and your preferred display currency (e.g. USD, EUR, INR, GBP). This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Cookie Policy

We may display non-invasive advertisements served by Google AdSense to fund our independent mathematical research and platform hosting. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our site or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

FinMetrics Pro is designed from first principles to comply fully with the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Because we do not store personal identifying data, there is zero risk of data exposure or third-party resale.
