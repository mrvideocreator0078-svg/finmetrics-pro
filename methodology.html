Calculation Methodology & Proofs

At FinMetrics Pro, every calculation is strictly grounded in classical actuarial science, financial economics, and published Federal Reserve guidelines. Below is the complete mathematical documentation of our core computational models.

1. Compound Interest with Continuous Regular Contributions

The future value of an investment portfolio compounded over multiple periods with periodic deposits is governed by the Future Value of an Annuity formula combined with the standard compounding formula:

Compound Future Value Equation
FV = P × (1 + r/n)^(nt) + PMT × [ ((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n) ]
Where P = Initial principal balance, PMT = Regular periodic contribution, r = Nominal annual rate of return, n = Number of compounding intervals per year, and t = Total duration in years.

2. Standard Mortgage Amortization & Accelerated Extra Principal Reduction

Residential fixed-rate mortgage payments are amortized such that each payment covers the exact monthly interest owed on the outstanding balance, with the remainder reducing the loan principal:

Monthly Principal & Interest Equation
M = P × [ r(1 + r)^N ] / [ (1 + r)^N - 1 ]
Where M is the fixed monthly payment, P is the starting loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate (APR / 12), and N is the total number of amortization months (e.g. 360 months for a 30-year term).

When extra principal ($P_{extra}$) is supplied, it directly reduces the ending balance of that specific month $t$:

Accelerated Balance Recalculation
Balance(t) = Balance(t-1) - [ PrincipalPayment(t) + ExtraPrincipal(t) ]

3. Automotive Lease Economics & Capitalized Cost Reductions

Automobile lease contracts break down monthly payments into two distinct components: Depreciation Fee and Finance (Rent) Fee:

Monthly Lease Payment Formula
Lease Payment = (Net Cap Cost - Residual Value) / Term + (Net Cap Cost + Residual Value) × Money Factor
Where Money Factor = Lease APR ÷ 2,400.
